About Me
I am an Electrical and Biomedical Engineering student at McMaster University, in Hamilton, Ontario, with the goal of working in hardware/software embedded systems. Thus, I strive to be multifaceted and adaptable to keep up with the depths of knowledge and rapid advancements that characterize the field.
To continuously improve on my technical and soft skills, I often seek new projects or tasks to help me learn and grow as a future engineer. This website is a detailed portfolio of my academic and professional expertise and proves my skills, as well as my commitment to self-improvement. In fact, I built this website myself, using skills I learned from the internet (Shoutout to The Odin Project!).
I hope that above all else, this portfolio demonstrates my willingness and capability to learn and grow, take risks, make mistakes, and adapt to new challenges!
